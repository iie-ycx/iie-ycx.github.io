Chenxu Yang (杨晨旭)

Ph.D. candidate @ IIE, CAS | VLM & RL & Inference Efficiency

I am a Ph.D. candidate at the Institute of Information Engineering, Chinese Academy of Sciences in Beijing, advised by Prof. Zheng Lin. I am currently interning at JD Explore Academy (京东探索研究院) through the TGT Program, advised by Qingyi Si, Jiaqi Wang and Nan Duan, focusing on Multimodal Understanding, Post-training (RL), and Efficient Reasoning. I have published 20+ peer-reviewed papers in top-tier AI conferences including ICLR, NeurIPS, ACL, EMNLP, AAAI, IJCAI, and COLING (including collaborative publications), with 15 papers as first or co-first author.
